1. System Overview & Digital Twin Architecture

Land8Fire is a high-performance 3D geospatial Wildfire Digital Twin engineered with React 18, TypeScript, and CesiumJS. It couples real-time meteorological observations with an authoritative Rothermel (1972) surface fire spread physics engine and a multispectral deep learning U-Net model for burned area segmentation.

Role of the Environmental Parameters Panel

The Environmental Parameters Panel is not merely an informational dashboard; it provides the thermodynamic and fluid-dynamic boundary conditions directly consumed by the fire propagation differential equations. Every parameter dynamically modulates the rate and geometry of the flame front.

2. Mathematical & Physical Foundation: Rothermel Model

Wildfire spread is fundamentally governed by heat transfer balance: the rate at which heat is released in the reaction zone versus the heat required to ignite adjacent unburned fuel ($Q_{ig}$).

// Directional Rate of Spread (ROS) across 36 sectors (Delta Theta = 10 deg) ROS(θ) = ROS_0 × Burnability × η_M × TempBoost × φ_wind(θ) × φ_slope(θ) // Where: ROS_0 = Baseline forward spread rate of the fuel bed (~2.5 m/min) Burnability = ESA WorldCover / Fuel model coefficient (0.0 to 1.0) η_M = Rothermel Fuel Moisture Damping Coefficient TempBoost = Ambient preheating factor: max(0.5, 1 + (T_ambient - 20) / 40) φ_wind(θ) = Directional wind modifier: max(0.1, 1 + c_w × U_wind × cos(θ - θ_wind)) φ_slope(θ) = Terrain slope gradient modifier: max(0.2, 1 + c_s × tan(slope) × cos(θ - aspect))

3. Environmental Parameters: Physics & Propagation Impact

Parameter Metric / Units Physical Mechanism & Impact on Fire Spread
Temperature Degree Celsius (°C) Preheating of Fine Fuels: Cellulosic forest fuels must reach pyrolysis temperature (~300°C–320°C) to ignite. Higher ambient temperature reduces the enthalpy deficit:
Q_ig = C_p × (T_ignite - T_ambient) + M_f × ΔH_vap.
Less combustion heat is wasted preheating the fuel bed, accelerating forward spread.
Relative Humidity Percentage (%) Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD): Governs atmospheric drying suction. Low RH (<30%) rapidly extracts water from dead leaves and needles. High RH drives moisture absorption, dampening ignition potential.
1-Hour Dead Fuel Moisture (EMC) Percentage (%)
Simard / Rothermel		 Latent Heat of Vaporization Barrier: Water absorbs 2,260 kJ/kg to vaporize. As fuel moisture rises, Rothermel damping factor η_M drops precipitously:
η_M = 1 - 2.59(M_f/M_x) + 5.11(M_f/M_x)² - 3.52(M_f/M_x)³.
At M_x = 30% (Moisture of Extinction), η_M = 0 and fire spread stops entirely.
Wind Speed & Direction km/h & Bearing (°)
Compass Vector		 Forced Convection & Flame Tilting: Wind tilts the buoyant flame geometry forward over unburned vegetation, exponentially increasing radiant flux and preheating. Transforms circular spread into an elongated ellipse along the wind vector.
Precipitation Millimeters (mm) Direct Surface Quenching: Liquid rain coats fuel surfaces, driving effective moisture beyond extinction (e^(-0.6 × precip) damping), arresting the flame front.
Surface Pressure hPa / Barometric Atmospheric Stability & Winds: High-pressure systems (>1013 hPa) generate sinking air (subsidence), resulting in compression warming, low humidity, and high wildfire danger.

4. Ember Spotting & River Crossing Mechanics

A critical capability of the Land8Fire Digital Twin is distinguishing between direct surface flame propagation and aerodynamic ember spotting (river crossing):

1. Water Boundary Clamping (ESA WorldCover 10m)

As each of the 36 sector rays expands outward, the simulation queries high-resolution 10m land-cover classification. When a sector ray reaches a water body (river, lake, wetland), direct surface fire spread is strictly clamped at the water’s edge (0 m/min).

2. Downwind Ember Ballistics & Spot Fire Ignition

In strong winds, burning firebrands (embers) are lofted into the convection column and transported downwind. Our model calculates the maximum aerodynamic jump reach:

Max_Spotting_Distance ≈ U_wind × 35 × cos(θ - θ_wind) × (1.4 - RH / 100)

If the opposite bank of the river is burnable forest land and lies within this reach, the Digital Twin spawns a secondary spot fire with its own expanding elliptical perimeter, accurately modeling real-world river crossings.

6. Anticipated Panel Questions & Authoritative Answers

Q1: Why use 36 radial sectors instead of a standard 2D grid cellular automata?
Answer: Cellular automata suffer from grid bias (distorting diagonal spread). The 36-sector vector-front formulation preserves true continuous compass azimuths, allows precise ellipsoidal modeling under shifting wind vectors, and renders natively at 60 FPS in Cesium 3D with zero rasterization latency.
Q2: How does the Simard-Rothermel EMC differ from Live Canopy Moisture?
Answer: Live fuel moisture changes slowly across seasons (deciduous vs. evergreen), whereas 1-Hour Dead Fuel Moisture responds within 60 minutes to hourly ambient temperature and relative humidity. It is the single most volatile factor in determining flashover and surface fire acceleration.
Q3: How is this validated against real-world wildfire data?
Answer: The Digital Twin includes the Copernicus EMSR239 benchmark disaster event and real Amazon wildfire observations, comparing simulated perimeters against Copernicus Emergency Management ground-truth delimitation maps and Sentinel-2 multispectral U-Net predictions.
